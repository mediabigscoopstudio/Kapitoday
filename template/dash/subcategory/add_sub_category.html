Help Desk

A Sub Category sits under a Parent Category and helps organise products more precisely. For example, under Ground Coffee, you would create sub categories like Filter Coffee Powder, Espresso Ground Coffee, or Cold Brew Ground Coffee. This improves navigation for customers and helps Google index your pages more accurately.

Use the exact term customers search for. For example, Filter Coffee Powder is better than FC Powder. Search-friendly sub category titles like Single Origin Ground Coffee, Decaf Ground Coffee, or Organic Ground Coffee drive targeted traffic and rank for specific buyer intent queries.

Keep it under 60 characters and include the grind type and your brand. Examples:
  • Buy Filter Coffee Powder Online – Kapi Today
  • Fresh Espresso Ground Coffee – Kapi Today
  • Single Origin Coffee – Coorg & Chikmagalur | Kapi Today

Use specific, intent-driven keywords per sub category. Examples:
  • Filter Coffee Powder: filter coffee powder, South Indian filter coffee, decoction coffee powder
  • Cold Brew: cold brew ground coffee, coarse grind coffee, cold brew coffee India
  • Single Origin: Coorg coffee, Chikmagalur ground coffee, Araku valley coffee, single estate coffee India
  • Organic: organic ground coffee India, chemical-free coffee, natural roasted coffee

Use a focused product-style image that represents the grind type — e.g., a close-up of fine filter powder for Filter Coffee Powder, or a Moka pot with coarse grounds for Moka Pot Ground Coffee. Recommended size is 800×600px or larger in JPG or PNG. A relevant image improves customer trust and time-on-page.

Sub categories are filtered to only show Enabled parent categories to prevent orphaned sub categories from being accidentally linked to a disabled or inactive category. If you cannot find your parent category in the dropdown, enable it first from the Category list.
