Our Belief

Coffee wasn't born in a café. It was born on a plantation floor, filtered through brass, and poured with love.

Kapi Today exists to bring that real, unhurried South Indian coffee experience to every corner of the world. No shortcuts. No compromises. Just the way your grandmother made it.

Know Your Roast

The Roast Spectrum

Light Roast
Citrus · Floral · Bright Acidity
Delicate and complex. Best for pour-over and cold brew. Lets the estate's terroir sing.
Medium Roast
Chocolate · Caramel · Balanced
The all-rounder. Perfect for South Indian filter. Rich body, smooth finish, zero bitterness.
Dark Roast
Smoky · Bold · Full Body
Intense and unapologetic. Made for espresso and strong filter decoction. Not for the faint.
Monsoon Malabar
Earthy · Spicy · Low Acidity
Exposed to monsoon winds on the Malabar coast. A uniquely Indian roast found nowhere else on Earth.
