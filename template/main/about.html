How It Started

It Started with a Grandmother's Filter Coffee

Every morning, without fail, she would fill the brass filter with freshly ground coffee from the estate down the hill. The decoction would drip slowly — as if it knew there was no reason to rush. Mixed with hot milk and just enough jaggery, poured between the davara and tumbler until it was frothy and perfect.

"We grew up believing that's just what coffee was. Then we traveled the world and realized — nobody knew this coffee existed."

That's the frustration that birthed Kapi Today. The world's third-largest coffee producer, yet South Indian coffee remains invisible on the global stage. We decided to change that — not by adapting to the world's taste, but by bringing the world to ours.

Unapologetically authentic. No dilution. No westernization. Just real South Indian kapi, exactly as it was always meant to be — now delivered to your doorstep, anywhere on Earth.







What We Stand For

Three Pillars. Zero Compromise.

Unapologetically Authentic
We don't soften our coffee for foreign palates. If it's filter coffee, it's brewed through brass with full decoction strength. If it's a dark roast, it's bold enough to stand up to milk and jaggery. This is how South India drinks coffee — and now, so can you.
Estate to Cup
Every bean is traceable to a specific family-run estate in the Western Ghats. No blending from anonymous commodity lots. We know the farmer, the altitude, the soil. We roast within 24 hours of your order. The freshest coffee you'll ever taste.
Culture, Not Just Coffee
Kapi Today isn't selling a beverage — we're sharing a culture. The davara-tumbler ritual, the morning filter ceremony, the red oxide veranda with brass everything. Every product we make carries a piece of South Indian heritage.






From Seed to Sip

The Journey of Your Kapi

01
Shade-Grown in the Ghats
Coffee plants grow under the canopy of silver oak and pepper vines at 1,000–1,400m altitude in the Western Ghats.
02
Hand-Picked at Peak
Only ripe red cherries are hand-selected by experienced pickers during harvest season — December to March.
03
Sun-Dried on Red Oxide
Cherries are spread on traditional red oxide drying yards under the South Indian sun. Slow and natural.
04
Freshly Roasted to Order
We roast in small batches within 24 hours of your order. Light, medium, dark, or monsoon — crafted to the profile.
05
Filtered Through Brass
Packed fresh and shipped worldwide. We even include brew guides so you can make the perfect davara-tumbler pour at home.
06
In Your Cup, Worldwide
From the mist-covered slopes of the Western Ghats to 50+ countries. The same authentic kapi — wherever you call home.
